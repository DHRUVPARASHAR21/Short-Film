# Generation Prompts

## Global Style Prompt
Mature hand-drawn historical anime film, naturalistic anatomy, restrained facial acting, expressive eyes and hands, painterly countryside backgrounds, subtle textured linework, muted earth-tone palette, cinematic natural lighting, soft atmospheric perspective, believable cloth and hair physics, no glossy CGI appearance. Timeless pre-industrial rural setting. Poetic but grounded. Emotional storytelling through composition and gesture rather than exaggerated expressions. Every shot belongs to the same continuous film and must preserve established character identity, wardrobe logic, environment geography, butterfly design, and rendering style.

## Sequence I — The Chase
Begin with an extreme macro close-up of a small pale ivory-white butterfly resting on sunlit meadow grass at dawn. A five-year-old boy's hand slowly enters frame and reaches toward it. The butterfly suddenly lifts away. Reveal the same boy standing barefoot in the field, briefly disappointed, then breaking into an innocent smile before running after it.

Follow low beside his bare feet through tall grass. He jumps for the butterfly and falls backward into the meadow. Brief overhead image: the boy lying happily beneath an enormous blue sky while the butterfly crosses above him.

Use a fluid visual time transition: as he rises from the grass, he is several years older. He runs past his mother outside the cottage without noticing her reaching gently toward him. Continue his run toward the old tree. As he passes behind the tree, transform him naturally into a seventeen-year-old version of the same person.

His youthful playfulness has become determination. He nearly catches the butterfly. It abruptly changes direction.

He stops.

Beneath the old tree stands a young woman.

Slow the camera dramatically.

She looks at him rather than the butterfly.

End on their first silent eye contact.

Keep all transformations elegant and readable rather than surreal. Hair, clothing, body proportions, and environment must remain coherent. Early camera movement energetic and low; final moment nearly still.

## Sequence II — Love
Continue directly from the first eye contact beneath the old tree. Wind gently moves one strand of the young woman's hair. The butterfly drifts between them, but neither person looks at it.

Focus on their hands hanging uncertainly by their sides. Their fingertips approach and finally touch.

Match cut from joined hands to them walking together through the same field.

Compress years through elegant visual montage. They lift a wooden beam together. Stone walls rise. Roof timbers appear. The finished modest rural cottage stands in warm evening light with smoke coming from the chimney.

Show the couple seated outside, exhausted and content, her head resting against his shoulder. The butterfly briefly lands on the roof unnoticed.

Transition indoors into warm firelight. The now-young-adult man holds a newborn baby carefully against his chest, initially nervous and unsure. The baby's tiny hand closes around his finger.

His face softens completely.

Reveal his wife watching him.

Their eyes meet.

End on the three of them occupying one warm composition.

This is the emotional warmth peak of the film. Camera movement gentle, stable, and intimate. Color palette shifts toward amber, burgundy, wood brown, and soft gold.

## Sequence III — Time
Begin on the newborn's hand gripping the father's finger. Match transition into a toddler holding both parents' hands while learning to walk.

Accelerate time through smooth visual rhymes. The toddler becomes a six-year-old child running toward his father. The father lifts him high into the air. Transition to father and son lying together in tall grass watching clouds.

The white butterfly crosses high above them, but the father does not move toward it.

Transition naturally to the son as a teenager sitting at the family's wooden table. The father is visibly older now and watches him with quiet awareness.

Morning. The son stands outside with a small travel bag.

His mother gently fixes his collar even though it does not need fixing.

The father offers his hand formally.

The son ignores the handshake and embraces him instead.

The father initially freezes, then holds him tightly.

The son walks down the familiar road and raises a hand goodbye.

The father raises his own hand.

Match cut to the dinner table.

Two occupied places.

One empty chair.

Transition into autumn: the now-grey-haired husband and wife walking slowly through the same field where they first met.

She stumbles slightly.

He catches her.

She silently laughs.

End with them continuing to walk together.

The visual rhythm should accelerate through childhood and slow abruptly during the goodbye and final elderly walk.

## Sequence IV — Release
Morning light enters the cottage bedroom.

The elderly man's hand slowly reaches toward the other side of the bed.

It is empty.

Do not show death.

Cut to quiet household objects: the wife's hairbrush, a dried flower, two cups with only one used.

Through the window, the familiar white butterfly passes.

Winter.

The elderly man walks slowly alone into the same field, now covered in snow.

The butterfly drifts toward him.

His old instinct returns.

His trembling hand begins reaching after it.

He stops himself.

Close on the wrinkled hand—the same compositional shape as the child's hand at the beginning.

The butterfly gently lands on his open palm.

He looks at it without triumph.

Recognition.

His eyes become slightly wet.

A tiny peaceful smile.

He opens his fingers and voluntarily lets the butterfly leave.

Do not make this magical or melodramatic.

Transition the white snow around his resting hand into small white spring flowers growing through green grass.

Spring has returned.

A new little boy runs into frame chasing the same butterfly.

The child continues running toward the distance.

For the first time in the entire film, the camera stays completely still and refuses to follow him.

End on moving grass, distant child, old tree, cottage, and butterfly disappearing toward sunlight.

Cut to black.
