# Visual Continuity Bible

## Protagonist

### Child
- messy brown hair
- warm brown eyes
- rounder face
- beige linen shirt
- simple trousers
- barefoot
- open, curious posture

### Teen
- same eyes, nose, and hair identity
- leaner face
- longer limbs
- darker rural clothing
- more focused expression

### Adult
- recognizably same facial structure
- stronger jaw
- same eye shape and hairline
- slightly heavier rural clothing
- grounded, stable posture

### Old
- same nose, brow, and eye structure
- grey-white hair
- short beard
- deep wrinkles
- physically diminished but immediately recognizable

## Wife
- long light-brown / blonde hair
- warm, gentle eyes
- earth-red / burgundy clothing as recurring color cue
- understated performance
- no melodramatic expressions

## Son
Should plausibly inherit:
- father's eyes
- mother's softer facial proportions

## Butterfly
Always:
- small pale ivory-white butterfly
- realistic proportions
- slightly translucent wings
- no magical glow
- no species changes

## Environment
The geography must never change.

Lock:
- old tree
- mountain profile
- field
- path
- cottage
- horizon

Only season, weather, light, and state of the house may change.

## Global Visual Style
- mature hand-drawn historical anime
- naturalistic anatomy
- restrained facial acting
- expressive eyes and hands
- painterly countryside backgrounds
- subtle textured linework
- muted earth tones
- cinematic natural lighting
- soft atmospheric perspective
- believable cloth and hair physics
- timeless pre-industrial rural setting
- poetic but grounded

## Global Negative Constraints
- no dialogue
- no lip syncing
- no subtitles
- no on-screen text
- no logos
- no modern objects
- no modern architecture
- no random crowds
- no facial identity drift
- no changing eye colors
- no unexplained wardrobe changes
- no fantasy particles
- no glowing butterfly aura
- no chibi proportions
- no cartoon comedy expressions
- no exaggerated crying
- no hyperactive camera
- no glossy 3D / CG look
- no photoreal humans
- no random landscape changes
- no malformed hands or extra limbs
