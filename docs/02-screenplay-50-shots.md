# 50-Shot Silent Screenplay

## 1
Macro: pale butterfly resting on sunlit grass.

## 2
A small child's hand reaches toward it.

## 3
The butterfly escapes. Reveal the smiling boy.

## 4
He runs after it.

## 5
Bare feet crash through tall grass.

## 6
He leaps, misses, and falls.

## 7
He lies laughing beneath a huge sky as the butterfly crosses above.

## 8
He rises several years older.

## 9
He runs past his mother outside the cottage.

## 10
She watches him go.

## 11
He passes behind the old tree and emerges as a teenager.

## 12
He nearly catches the butterfly.

## 13
The butterfly changes direction. He stops.

## 14
A young woman stands beneath the tree.

## 15
For the first time, he forgets to chase.

## 16
Their eyes meet.

## 17
The butterfly passes between them; neither looks at it.

## 18
Their fingertips almost touch.

## 19
Their hands meet.

## 20
They walk away together.

## 21
They lift a wooden beam together.

## 22
A wall rises.

## 23
A roof appears.

## 24
They rest outside the finished house.

## 25
The butterfly lands on the roof, unnoticed.

## 26
The man holds a newborn.

## 27
The baby's tiny hand closes around his finger.

## 28
The wife watches father and child.

## 29
The baby becomes a toddler learning to walk.

## 30
The child grows; the father lifts him into the air.

## 31
Father and son lie in the grass watching clouds.

## 32
Teenage son sits at the wooden table.

## 33
The son stands with a travel bag.

## 34
The mother fixes his collar.

## 35
The father offers his hand; the son hugs him instead.

## 36
The father hugs him tightly.

## 37
The son walks down the road and turns.

## 38
The father raises his hand goodbye slightly too long.

## 39
Two bowls on the table; a third place is empty.

## 40
The wife places her hand over the father's.

## 41
The elderly couple walk through the field.

## 42
She stumbles; he catches her; she silently laughs.

## 43
Morning: the old man reaches toward the empty side of the bed.

## 44
His hand remains there.

## 45
Her hairbrush, dried flower, and two cups; only one is used.

## 46
Winter: the old man walks into the snowy field.

## 47
The butterfly approaches. His old instinct returns.

## 48
He looks at his wrinkled, trembling hand.

## 49
The butterfly lands in his palm. He smiles and releases it.

## 50
Spring returns. His old hand rests among flowers while another child runs after the same butterfly. The camera does not follow.
